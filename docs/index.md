# Welcome to MatStream Help

MatStream is a modern Product Lifecycle Management (PLM) platform designed 
for engineering teams. It helps you manage parts, documents, assemblies, 
and their full lifecycle — from creation through release and beyond.

- 🚀 [Quick start](getting-started/quick-start.md) — get up and running fast
- 🔧 [Entities](entities/overview.md) — learn how parts and documents are managed
- ⚙️ [Administration](admin/categories.md) — configure your workspace
