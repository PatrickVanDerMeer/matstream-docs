# MatStream

## The structured data and lifecycle management platform

MatStream gives your organisation a flexible, engineering-grade foundation 
for managing any type of item — parts, documents, complaints, suppliers, 
or products — with full versioning, revision control, configurable 
approval workflows, and relationship tracking between items.

Built for teams who need more than a spreadsheet, and less complexity 
than a traditional enterprise system.

---

## One platform, many use cases

MatStream's flexible core adapts to the way your organisation works. 
The same powerful foundation supports a wide range of use cases:

| Solution | What MatStream manages |
|---|---|
| [**Product Lifecycle Management**](solutions/plm.md) | Parts, assemblies, drawings, BOMs, and engineering change workflows |
| [**Document Control**](solutions/document-control.md) | Controlled documents with revision history and approval workflows |
| [**Quality Management**](solutions/quality-management.md) | Non-conformances, corrective actions, and quality records |
| [**Inventory Management**](solutions/inventory-management.md) | Products, stock levels, suppliers, and procurement data |

---

## What makes MatStream different?

Unlike generic platforms, MatStream is built with engineering rigour:

- **Entity-everything model** — any item type, fully configurable, 
  linked to any other item type
- **Engineering-grade versioning** — every change is tracked, every 
  formal release is labelled with a revision
- **Configurable lifecycle** — define your own states, transitions, 
  and approval gates
- **BOM traceability** — full uses and where-used relationships across 
  your entire catalogue
- **Autodesk Vault integration** — sync parts and assemblies from Vault 
  directly into MatStream
- **Multi-tenant SaaS** — your data is isolated, secure, and accessible 
  from any browser
