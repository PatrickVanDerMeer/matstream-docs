# Collaborate

The Collaborate page provides a structured view of your external collaboration relationships — customers, suppliers, and other third parties. It is designed to help teams track and manage cross-organisational interactions within MatStream.

!!! note "Feature status"
    The Collaborate page is currently in early development. The structure and navigation are in place; full data integration is being built in upcoming releases.

---

## Accessing Collaborate

Navigate to **Collaborate** from the main workspace navigation bar.

URL: `/w/{workspaceSlug}/collaborate`

---

## Collaboration structure

The left panel shows a tree with three top-level categories:

| Node | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Clients** | Customers or external parties you deliver work to |
| **Suppliers** | Vendors and sub-contractors you source from |
| **Third Parties** | Other external organisations (regulators, certification bodies, partners) |

Expand a node to see individual partners within that category. Select a partner to see their details in the right panel.

---

## What's coming

Planned capabilities for the Collaborate feature:

- Link entities, BOMs, and documents to specific collaboration partners
- Share selected entity data externally with controlled visibility
- Track status of shared items (e.g. "sent to supplier for quote", "awaiting approval")
- Audit trail of cross-organisation data exchanges
