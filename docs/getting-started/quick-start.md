# Quick Start

Welcome to MatStream. This guide walks you through creating your account, confirming your email, and accessing your first workspace.

---

## Step 1 — Create an account

Navigate to [app.matstream.com](https://app.matstream.com) and click **Create an account**.

Enter your email address and a password, then click **Register**. MatStream will send a confirmation email to the address you provided.

!!! note
    If you were invited to a workspace by an administrator, you may receive an invitation email directly. Click the link in that email to start the registration process with your email pre-filled.

---

## Step 2 — Confirm your email

Check your inbox for an email from **noreply@matstream.com** with the subject **Confirm your registration**.

Click the **Confirm registration** button in the email. You will be redirected to MatStream and your account will be activated automatically.

!!! tip
    If you don't see the email, check your spam or junk folder. You can also request a new confirmation email from the sign-in screen.

---

## Step 3 — Sign in

After confirmation, click **Login** to go to the sign-in screen.

Enter your email address and click **Next**. Then enter your password and click **Sign in**.

---

## Step 4 — Access your workspace

After signing in, MatStream checks whether you have a default workspace configured:

- **Default workspace set** — you are redirected automatically after a 5-second countdown. Click **Continue** to go immediately.
- **One workspace available** — you are redirected immediately.
- **Multiple workspaces available** — a picker is shown so you can choose which workspace to open.
- **No workspace yet** — see [Creating a workspace](workspaces.md).
