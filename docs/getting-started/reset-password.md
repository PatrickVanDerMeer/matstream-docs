# Reset your password

If you have forgotten your password, you can request a reset link 
from the sign in screen. The same steps set a first password for an 
account that was created with **Continue with Microsoft** or 
**Continue with Google** and never had one.

---

## Requesting a reset link

1. Go to the MatStream sign in page and enter your email address.
2. Click **Next** to proceed to the password screen.
3. Click **Forgot password?** below the sign in button.
4. Your email address is pre-filled. Click **Send Reset Link**.
5. MatStream sends a reset email to your address. The page confirms 
   this with a success message.

!!! note
    For security reasons, MatStream shows the same success message 
    whether or not the email address exists in the system. This 
    prevents exposing which addresses are registered.

---

## Setting a new password

1. Open the reset email from **MatStream &lt;noreply@matstream.com&gt;**.
2. Click **Reset password**.
3. Enter your new password and confirm it.
4. Click **Reset Password**.
5. MatStream confirms the change and redirects you to the sign in 
   page after a few seconds.

### Password requirements

MatStream requires passwords to meet the following rules:

- At least 8 characters
- At least one uppercase letter (A–Z)
- At least one lowercase letter (a–z)
- At least one number (0–9)
- At least one special character (e.g. `!`, `@`, `#`, `$`)

---

## Reset link expiry

The reset link in the email is valid for **1 hour**. If the link 
has expired, go back to the sign in screen and request a new one.

Each reset link can only be used once. After you set a new password, 
the link becomes invalid.

---

## Changing your password when signed in

If you are already signed in and want to change your password:

1. Click your profile or account settings
2. Navigate to **Change Password**
3. Enter your current password, then your new password twice
4. Click **Change Password**

The same password complexity rules apply.
