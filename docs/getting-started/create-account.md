# Create an account

This page explains how to create a MatStream account, confirm your 
email address, and sign in for the first time — with a password or 
with your Microsoft or Google account.

---

## Register

Navigate to [app.matstream.com](https://app.matstream.com) and click 
**Create an account**.

!!! tip "Prefer not to manage another password?"
    You can skip registration entirely and click **Continue with 
    Microsoft** or **Continue with Google** on the sign-in screen. 
    MatStream creates your account from the verified email address 
    of that provider — no confirmation email, no password. See 
    [Sign in with Microsoft or Google](#sign-in-with-microsoft-or-google).

Enter your email address and choose a password, then click **Register**.

!!! note "Invited by an administrator?"
    If a workspace administrator has added you to their workspace, you 
    will receive an invitation email with a registration link. Click 
    the link in that email — your email address will be pre-filled on 
    the registration page.

---

## Confirm your email

After registering, MatStream sends a confirmation email from 
**noreply@matstream.com** with the subject **Confirm your registration**.

Open the email and click **Confirm registration**. Your account is 
activated immediately and you are redirected to the sign-in screen.

!!! tip
    If the email does not arrive within a few minutes, check your spam 
    or junk folder. If it is not there either, go to the sign-in screen, 
    enter your email address, and click **Resend Confirmation Email**.

---

## Sign in

On the sign-in screen, enter your email address and click **Next**. 
Then enter your password and click **Sign in**.

MatStream checks your workspace access and redirects you automatically. 
See [Accessing a workspace](workspace-access.md) for details on what 
happens after sign-in.

---

## Sign in with Microsoft or Google

Instead of a password you can sign in with a Microsoft account (work, 
school or personal) or a Google account. On the sign-in screen, click 
**Continue with Microsoft** or **Continue with Google**, choose the 
account you want to use, and approve the request the first time.

MatStream links the provider account to a MatStream account by email 
address:

| Situation | What happens |
|---|---|
| **You already have a MatStream account with that email** | It is linked; you land in your workspace as usual. Your password keeps working alongside. |
| **No MatStream account with that email yet** | A new account is created, already confirmed. No password is set. |
| **The provider cannot confirm the email address** | Sign-in is refused. Sign in with your password once, then use the provider button — it links to the account you are signed in with. |

After the first sign-in the link is remembered: MatStream recognises 
the provider account itself, even if you later change the email 
address at the provider.

!!! note "Accounts without a password"
    An account created through Microsoft or Google has no MatStream 
    password. You do not need one — 
    [MatStreamConnect](../integrations/matstream-connect.md) offers the 
    same provider buttons — but if you ever want one, use 
    **Forgot password?** on the sign-in screen to set one.

!!! note "Wrong account chosen?"
    The provider always shows an account picker, so you can choose 
    between a work and a personal account. If you end up in the wrong 
    MatStream account, sign out and pick the other one.

---

## Forgot your password?

On the sign-in screen, click **Forgot password?** after entering your 
email address. MatStream sends a password reset link to your email 
address. The link is valid for one hour.
