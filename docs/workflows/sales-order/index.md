# Sales Order Workflow

Follow a customer order across Sales, Procurement, Incoming Goods, and Dispatch. Open the guide for your department, or follow the steps below from order creation through shipment and invoice-document preparation.

## Department steps

1. **Sales — [Create a Sales Order](../create-sales-order.md).** Create and price the customer order, check availability, then confirm demand.
2. **Procurement — [Create a PO from Material Requirements](../arrange-missing-stock.md).** Arrange missing stock, review the linked supplier orders, and issue them.
3. **Procurement — [Share a PO with a vendor](../vendor-purchase-order.md).** Provide the issued PO, applicable documents, and supplier portal link.
4. **Supplier — [Use the supplier portal](../supplier-portal.md).** Download the PO and applicable documents, and upload required vendor documents such as material certificates.
5. **Incoming Goods — [Complete a supplier delivery](../receive-warehouse-goods.md).** Print a Receiving List, check the delivery, receive the outstanding quantities, and verify stock.
6. **Dispatch — [Pick, pack, and ship the order](../pick-pack-send.md).** Print Pick and Packing Lists, mark the order picked and packed, then confirm shipment.
7. **Sales — [Prepare invoice documents](../../features/sales-orders.md#5-create-the-sales-order-invoice-document).** Create the Sales Order invoice document.

## Setup and walkthroughs

The Sales, Procurement, Incoming Goods, and Dispatch guides include movies using one connected example. Receiving and dispatch demonstrate complete deliveries.

See [whole-order setup and behavior](../whole-order-pick-pack-receive.md) for configuration and the complete-delivery process.
