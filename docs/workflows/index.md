# Work Flows

Follow a process from start to finish to get something done in MatStream.

Each workflow gives you the steps in the order you need them: where to start, what to do, and what result to expect. A workflow can take you across several features, such as sales orders, stock, purchasing, and shipping.

Detailed topic pages explain individual features, fields, and settings. Workflow guides keep those details brief and link to topic pages when you need more information.

## Available workflows

### Inventor assembly and BOM workflow

Create an assembly with existing fittings and a new part, fill in its information in Inventor, create a drawing with a parts list, and publish the drawing and BOM to MatStream using iLogic.

[Follow the Inventor workflow](inventor-assembly-bom.md)

### Sales Orders and invoice documents

Sales creates a customer order, adds and prices its articles, checks stock demand, confirms the order, and prepares the draft invoice document.

[Follow the Sales workflow](../features/sales-orders.md)

### Arrange missing stock

Procurement reviews shortages and replenishment requirements, creates supplier Purchase Orders, and issues the orders before handing the delivery information to Warehouse.

[Follow the Procurement workflow](arrange-missing-stock.md)

### Receive goods into the warehouse

Warehouse receiving matches a supplier delivery to its PO, records the quantity accepted into a storage location, and checks the outstanding quantities and stock ledger.

[Follow the Receiving workflow](receive-warehouse-goods.md)

### Open orders picking packing and sending

Warehouse dispatch finds confirmed Sales Orders, records picking and packing, confirms full or partial shipment, and checks the remaining backorder.

[Follow the Dispatch workflow](pick-pack-send.md)

### Supplier portal workflow

Receive a Purchase Order, download the PO and applicable documents, and upload required vendor documents such as material certificates.

[Follow the supplier workflow](supplier-portal.md)

### Share a PO with a vendor

Prepare and share a Purchase Order with its documents and supplier portal link.

[Follow the buyer workflow](vendor-purchase-order.md)

## How to use a workflow

1. Check **Before you start** for setup and access requirements.
2. Follow the numbered steps in order.
3. Check the result of each step before continuing.
4. Open linked topic help when you need more detail, then return to the workflow.

Your workspace may have different field names or actions if its configuration has been customized.
