# Upgrading your plan

Upgrading from Free to a paid plan requires completing a one-time 
payment setup. After that, all future payments happen automatically.

---

## Who can upgrade

Only the workspace **owner** can change the subscription plan.

---

## How to upgrade

1. Open **MatStream Manage** and go to your workspace
2. Click **Change plan** on the workspace card
3. Select the plan you want to upgrade to
4. Enter the number of **editor seats** you need

    !!! tip "How many seats do I need?"
        One seat = one editor. The workspace owner always has a seat. 
        Add one seat for each additional person who needs to create or 
        edit entities. Viewers do not need a seat.

5. Check the live cost preview — it shows your monthly total and the 
   amount due today for your first full billing month
6. Click **Confirm change**
7. You are redirected to the MatStream payment page

---

## The payment page

The payment page shows a summary of your first invoice, which covers 
a full month from your payment date.

Click **Pay** to be taken to our secure payment provider (Mollie). 
You will enter your card or bank details there — MatStream never 
sees or stores your payment information.

After completing the payment:

- Your plan is activated immediately
- Your payment date becomes your monthly billing anchor
- Your payment method is saved for automatic future billing
- You receive a confirmation email

---

## First invoice

Your first invoice covers a full month from the date you pay — not 
a prorated fraction of the current calendar month.

**Example:** You upgrade on the 20th of March.
3 editor seats × €19.95/month  = €59.85
Shared hosting                 =  €9.95
Subtotal                       = €69.80
VAT 21%                        = €14.66
Total first payment            = €84.46

Your next invoice is generated on the 20th of April, and every 
month thereafter on the 20th.

---

## Automatic recurring billing

After your first payment, MatStream charges your saved payment method 
automatically on your billing date each month. You will receive an 
invoice by email 7 days before each charge as a reminder.

You do not need to do anything — the charge happens in the background.

---

## Changing your seat count

You can increase or decrease your editor seat count at any time from 
the **Change plan** dialog.

**Adding seats** — takes effect immediately. The extra seats are 
prorated from today to your next billing date and added to your 
next invoice.

**Removing seats** — takes effect from the next billing period. 
No refund is issued for the current period. If you have more active 
editors than your new seat count allows, you will need to revoke 
access from some editors before the change takes effect.

---

## Upgrading between paid plans

If you upgrade from one paid plan to another (for example, Team to 
Professional), the difference in cost is prorated for the remaining 
days in your current billing period and charged immediately. Your 
billing date does not change.

---

## Downgrading to Free

You can downgrade back to the Free plan from **Change plan**. 
The downgrade takes effect at the end of your current billing period. 
Your current plan features remain active until that date.

!!! warning
    The Free plan allows only 1 editor (the owner). Before downgrading, 
    ensure your workspace has no other active editors assigned. 
    You can only have one Free workspace — if you already have one, 
    you must delete it before downgrading a second workspace.
