## How we work

The workflow itself is the test: quotation → project → engineering/approval → procurement → production → handover. At each step record what works, what needs configuration and what needs development. Supporting automated tests do not replace the practical walkthrough.

Every new business setting/default, mapping, label and role choice stays configurable by the workspace administrator through application UI. Use editable scripts where possible, backed by platform permissions, current-version checks, transactions, audit and durable events. Project teams maintain operational records, including Distribution Channels, within their permissions. Subscription choices also remain personal user settings.

**Status meanings:** “Implemented and locally tested” means the feature has recorded local evidence; it does not mean the complete real-world process or every user role has been rehearsed. “Still to validate” is not an assumed software defect. No production/internet deployment or real supplier payment is claimed.

## Agreed boundaries retained

- One enabled RVD occurrence category; multiple-category routing and Setup rerun tracking are not P0 gaps. Installed administrator customizations must still be preserved.
- Technical drawing review/approval/release takes place in Inventor/Vault. Engineer responsibility, permissions and history remain the controls; no mandatory different checker or automatic proof-of-linked-transmittals gate is added.
- Engineer edits the transmittal filter. Admins define defaults/permissions. Different content sets/purposes use separate transmittals; issued versions and attributed responses remain traceable.
- Accepted supplier documents use existing state permissions for read-only access; Quality reopens through authorised transitions. No additional locking engine.
- Customer Invoice Issued, supplier Paid, physical Received and technical Complete are separate. Scripts do not execute payments.
- Keep ordinary categories/Uses tabs/links and SQL int identifiers, with PropertyValueID remaining long/bigint.
